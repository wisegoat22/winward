    HOW YOUR APPROACH BECOMES TRAINING
    Goal first. Deliberate effort.
    1. Make the win explicit. Check the goal and the facts that affect it.
    2. Consider consequences. Search legal action sequences up to five steps, using their declared effects.
    3. Spend effort where it helps. Prefer efficient successful paths and stop when the goal is achieved.
    The model receives structured facts and action effects, not emotions or persuasive wording. The five-step reference is a bounded search, not a claim to have analyzed every possible real-world consequence.
