01 / TWO EXPLICIT RESEARCH TRACKS

Different paths. Separate evidence.

LOCAL · OWN WEIGHTS

TRAINED POLICY GROWTH

Grow the decision policy we already own.

Start from our trained 4.86M-parameter v4 policy and expand its width. The input remains 505 structured features per candidate. Growth aims to retain existing choices; it does not add new knowledge, remove v4’s known failures, or read ordinary text.

    Growth target: 4,251,056,641 parameters. The source reference is the existing v4 architecture; a new growth stage is reached only when its own run reports record initialization or updates.

    SCRATCH WORKFLOW READER

    Learn to read a small workflow language.

    A separate decoder starts from random weights and our byte tokenizer. Its first lessons teach goal completion, legal actions, and token/time costs. This is a narrow structured language, not general language pretraining.

      Planned target: 4,027,579,392 parameters. The latest completed 32M primitive pilot reached 70.31% at its best validation checkpoint (67.97% at the final step), against a 29.30% majority baseline. These are development results, not a final audit.

      “Weights updated” means optimizer steps were recorded. A capacity probe, larger parameter count, or saved checkpoint does not mean a stage passed a quality gate.
